THE WORKFLOW

From a list to
reviewable service areas.

  1. Load your service addresses.

    The app reads the collection day from the rows, worksheet or filename. Mixed-day files require a day selection in Upload options. You can also load the existing Monday or Tuesday list. Optional CollectionAccess values alley or curbside supply confirmed collection evidence.

  2. Enter your preferred range.

    The app builds natural areas first, then scores useful merges and supported divisions. Mid-block cuts between neighbouring homes and separating opposite sides of an alley carry a heavy penalty. Boundaries following mapped roads through intersections are valid. Existing satisfactory neighbours stay intact, and count exceptions remain visible.

  3. Inspect and export.

    Click inside an area for its name and count. Click an address for its service details. Search IDs, review checks and export assignments and boundaries. All processing and files stay local.

These are provisional planning sections. Truck routes are calculated separately. Pickup access and legal crossings remain unverified. Other-weekday gaps are not evidence of empty land.
